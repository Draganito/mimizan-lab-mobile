In Color there are two lamps under the paper strip, red and blue: the colour balance of the print, as on the desktop. A drag moves a lamp from where it stands, a double tap is zero. Burnt-out highlights are no longer cut off at the clip: the negative keeps what stands above it, the paper prints it, and the strip can bring a too-light sky down. A print in sRGB is written on the sRGB curve. The core is 0.18.16.
